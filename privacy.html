Partydrome privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

Partydrome is a party game. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and it does not sell or share anything about you.

On your phone

The app saves your chosen name, character, skin and game settings on your own phone, so they are there next time. Removing the app removes them.

Playing together ("Join game")

When you play online, your phone sends the name and character you chose, and your moves, to the game server so the other players can see them. These are kept in the server's memory only while the game exists: a room is deleted after six hours with nobody connected, and after 24 hours at the most. They are not written to disk or used for anything else.

Internet address

The game server counts connections per internet address for up to one minute, to stop people guessing game codes. Addresses are not written anywhere.

One-phone play ("Group solo")

Sends nothing anywhere.

Suggestions and bug reports

These open your own email app. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and we see only what is in that email, including your email address. We use it to read your idea or fix the bug, and to reply.

Children

Partydrome is meant for teens and adults, and does not knowingly collect anything from children.
